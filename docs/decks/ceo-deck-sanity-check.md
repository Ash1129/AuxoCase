# CEO deck sanity check

`AuxoCase_CEO.pptx`, 22 slides with notes, checked against the system's numbers and code on 2 Nov 2026. The arithmetic on slides 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 15, 18 and 19 checks out. Listed below are the places where the deck contradicts itself or the code. The first six are worth fixing before the CEO sees it.

## Fix before presenting

1. **Slides 3 and 4, notes: marketing does not approve the notes reader's change.** The ±10-point nudge applies automatically once code has found its quotes in the record. Marketing sees *flags*, from a separate reader, in Leads to check, and a flag never changes a score. Suggested wording: "it must quote the record word for word, code checks every quote, and it can move a score at most 10 points; a separate reader flags odd records for marketing to look at, without touching the score." Also say "marketing", not "a marketing agent", which sounds like an AI.
2. **Slide 13 contradicts itself and slide 12.** The slide asks for one measured month judged on one number, demos per 1,000 leads. The notes ask for "3 measured month" judged on two numbers, demos and closings per 1,000 leads. Slide 12 has one measured month, in month 2. Pick one version. If closings are in, note that one month is too short to see many closes, since demo to close takes weeks.
3. **Slide 8 uses 180 demos "before", while slides 1 and 18 use 172.** The 12-month history averages 172. At 172 the before column becomes 34 customers and $826k. The changes a month become +$2.29M at a 20% close rate, +$2.75M at 23% and +$3.02M with the lift. SDR hours per customer before becomes 7.3 on slide 9. Alternatively, keep 180 everywhere and say where it comes from.
4. **Slide 8, notes: "if we follow the same 20%, it amounts to 649 demos" is wrong.** The 649 demos are what was booked. The 20% is the share of demos that close. Suggested wording: "1,128 leads worked and 649 demos booked a month; at the old 20% close rate that is 130 customers."
5. **Slides 1 and 8 headline the assumed case.** The headline uses the 24.7% close rate: 160 customers and +$2.98M. That figure applies a booking lift to closing, which nothing measured. The simulation itself shows 23%: 149 customers and +$2.71M, or +$2.75M on a base of 172. A CEO who reads the footnote will ask why the headline isn't the measured number. Lead with 149, and show 160 as the upside.
6. **Slide 4: the notes say enrichment is agentic AI, but the graphic says it isn't.** The strap reads "GOLD OUTLINE = ONLY AI STEP" and only step 4 is gold. Outline step 1 in gold too and change the strap to "AI STEPS". In the notes, replace "An Agentic AI layer fills… by using AI agents to search online about the company" with: "the licensed tool first; for what it misses, Claude searches the web and must cite a page and quote it; code fetches the page and keeps the value only if the quote is there."

## Tidy up

7. **Slide 6 says five AI steps but the table shows four.** Reply triage is missing. Add a footnote: "Reply triage: a reply takes 4 min instead of 10; not counted in the capacity above."
8. **Slide 9 says the AI steps free about 290 SDR hours, but the team only has about 250.** Reword to "saves about 290 hours the same 1,128 leads would have cost". Be ready for a related question: 1,128 leads were worked against a stated capacity of 1,031. The title also wraps, leaving "6.9" alone on a line. "1.6 SDR hours per new customer, down from 6.9" fits.
9. **Slide 10, notes.** "175 tests" is now 193. After "the enrichment tool is already licensed", add "the web lookup behind it is about $10 a month".
10. **Slides 11 and 21 have no row for the web lookup.** Add "AI invents a title or size: must cite a page and quote it; code keeps it only if the quote is on the page", and make the title "Six risks". In slide 20's notes, the 539 of 748 filled came from the stand-in tool, not the web lookup; say so.
11. **Three demo orders.** Slide 7's screenshots run marketing, SDR, manager. Its notes run SDR, marketing, manager. Slide 22 runs SDR, manager, marketing, manager. Put slide 7's screenshots in the order you will click them, and make slide 22 match.
12. **Slide 2, notes: "we observed that a lead contacted within a day converts about twice as often".** That is BrightPath's own claim from the brief, which the simulation assumes. Slide 11's notes say so. Say "BrightPath's data shows" instead.
13. **Wording.** Slide 1 notes: "Discretion" should be "Disclaimer", and "AuxoCase, the product that I built," needs its commas. Slide 5 notes: "calibrated error" should be "calibration error". Slide 14 is "APPENDIX 1:" while the others are "APPENDIX:". On slides 9 and 19 the chart titles break "simu-lated" across lines. That may only happen in LibreOffice, so check it in PowerPoint.
