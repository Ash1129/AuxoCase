BrightPath Payroll Services (B2B SaaS payroll for 50-500-employee companies) needs a lead-scoring and routing system inside HubSpot. Marketing creates about 6,000 leads a month from six channels; HubSpot's points model passes about 1,500 MQLs to 12 SDRs, who can work only 500 and pick by gut feel, so good leads go cold and 1,000 are never contacted. Partner referrals and webinars convert 2-3x better but score low. The system must read lead and activity data from HubSpot continuously (hundreds of new leads a day), score and rank each lead by its likelihood to book a demo and close, assign it to an SDR, alert them, and write the score, rank, routing and a plain-English reason back into HubSpot. SDRs make the final call on who to contact. Feedback on which leads booked meetings and which closed must flow back into the scoring over time so marketing and sales share one number. Leads are business contacts (personal data). Budget $100-300k; three months to go live in HubSpot.
