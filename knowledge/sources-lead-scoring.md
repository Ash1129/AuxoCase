Blocked, paywalled or partial reads (claims limited accordingly):

- **duncan-2015-funnel**: ACM Digital Library returned 403 and Semantic Scholar lists the PDF as closed. The abstract was read from the EPA HERO bibliographic record (hero.epa.gov, reference 7164063) and cross-checked against the DBLP listing. Abstract only; the DQM/FFM internals (model form, features) are not cited.
- **nygard-2020-leadscoring**: ScholarSpace is behind a Cloudflare check and the AIS eLibrary PDF returned 403. Abstract and bibliographic details read from the AIS eLibrary landing page and the Åbo Akademi research portal. Abstract only; no quantitative results cited.
- **christodoulou-2019-lr-vs-ml**: ScienceDirect and PubMed blocked automated access. Abstract read via the Europe PMC REST API and Semantic Scholar. Abstract only.
- **niculescu-2005-calibration**: The Cornell-hosted PDF failed TLS verification, the ICML 2005 proceedings link is dead, CiteSeerX redirects to the Internet Archive (not fetchable). Abstract read from mlanthology.org. Abstract only; the learning-curve comparison of Platt vs isotonic data requirements is therefore not cited, only the per-model calibration findings stated in the abstract.
- **guo-2017-calibration**, **lundberg-2017-shap**, **gutierrez-2017-uplift**, **radcliffe-2011-uplift**, **bekker-2020-pu-survey**, **bottou-2013-counterfactual**, **wachter-2018-counterfactual**, **gama-2014-drift-survey**, **barocas-2016-disparate-impact**, **binns-2018-justice**, **dietvorst-2015-aversion**, **dietvorst-2018-overcoming**, **parasuraman-2010-automation-bias**, **homburg-2007-thought-worlds**, **jarvinen-2016-automation**, **aksin-2007-callcenter**, **ktena-2019-delayed-feedback**, **lee-2026-review**, **wp29-2018-adm-guidelines**: full-text PDFs were fetched and read via local text extraction (pdftotext). Section/page references in the chunks refer to the version read (preprint, accepted manuscript or working paper where noted in the table), so page numbers may differ from the publisher's typeset version.
- **lu-2018-drift-review**: read via the arXiv HTML rendering; the first ~100k characters (through Section VII) were read, the final appendix/reference section was not.
- **kotler-2006-hbr**: HBR serves only the opening section without a subscription. The four relationship types, the SLA recommendations and the funnel hand-off material are *not* cited; only the economic/cultural sources of conflict from the visible opening are. Treat this as a partial read.
- **oldroyd-2011-hbr**: HBR serves only the introduction. The commonly quoted audit figures (2,241 companies, 42-hour average response, 7x qualification odds within an hour) appear in secondary sources but were not verified against the article, so they are not cited. The BYU ScholarsArchive record confirms authorship and venue.
- **lrm-study-oldroyd**: vendor-hosted study page (InsideSales.com, no publication date stated). Figures are reported as the page states them; methodology (six companies, 15,000+ leads, business-hours exclusions) is as self-described. No peer-reviewed replication of the speed-to-lead effect was found despite two targeted searches; practitioner figures (XANT 5.7M-lead analysis, Velocify, Artemis) were seen only in search snippets and are not cited.
- **yan-2015-pipeline**: AAAI landing page read; abstract only.
- **zhang-2026-hpro**: arXiv landing page read; abstract only. Single-brand result; included as the most recent comparative evidence on pointwise vs ranking objectives.
- **gdpr-art22**: read from gdpr-info.eu (unofficial but verbatim consolidated text). Recitals 71/72 referenced there were not separately fetched.
- **Not found / not cited**: Chapelle (2014) "Modeling delayed feedback in display advertising" (KDD) — ACM blocked and the author's mirror returned 404; Ktena et al. (2019) was used instead. Gans, Koole & Mandelbaum (2003) call-center tutorial — all mirrors unreachable; Aksin et al. (2007) was used instead. No peer-reviewed field study of lead routing/assignment rules for sales teams was found; routing claims rest on the call-center operations literature.
