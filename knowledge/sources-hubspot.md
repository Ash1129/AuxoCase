### Uncertain or not verified from official pages
- Internal property names for deal flags (`hs_is_closed_won`, `hs_is_closed_lost`, `hs_closed_won_date`), lifecycle stage internal values (`marketingqualifiedlead`, `salesqualifiedlead`, etc.), `hs_lead_status`, and the enumeration values of `hs_analytics_source` were not found on an official page read today; the Knowledge Base articles give display labels only. Chunks use display labels in claims; before coding, confirm internal names with `GET /crm/v3/properties/{object}` on the target account.
- Two official pages disagree on private-app burst limits with the API limit increase: the usage guidelines say 250 per 10 seconds, the legacy private-apps page says 200. Both figures are recorded in H05.
- Two official pages disagree on webhook support for private apps: the private-apps page says webhooks are supported (subscriptions managed in app settings), the webhooks guide says the subscription APIs work only with legacy public apps. H04 records both; the practical reading is that private apps get webhooks without API-managed subscriptions.
- Webhook signature: the 2026-03 webhooks guide documents `X-HubSpot-Signature` (SHA-256 of app secret + body). It does not mention v3 signatures or request-timestamp headers; verify the signature version shown in the app's settings.
- The webhooks guide says `eventId` is a field; the project-based configuration page says `eventId` is not guaranteed unique. H04 recommends deduplicating on a composite key.
- Meeting outcome enumeration: the meetings API guide lists the outcomes in prose (scheduled, completed, rescheduled, no show, canceled) and shows only `SCHEDULED` in uppercase; the exact internal values of the others were not confirmed.
- The CCPA page states the B2B/employee exemptions "expired on December 31, 2022"; the task brief's "January 1, 2023" is the same moment expressed as the first day they no longer applied.
- HubSpot's predictive-scoring article gives no refresh frequency or minimum data requirement; third-party claims about these were not used.
- GDPR delete API scope requirements were empty on the reference page read; confirm the scope in the app's scope picker.
